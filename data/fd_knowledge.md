## SECTION 01: Fact sheet (all key details in one place)

The XYZ Long-Term Fixed Deposit from XYZ Bank has these key details.
The interest rate is four percent per year.
The tenure is from twenty years to forty years.
The minimum deposit is ten thousand rupees.
Interest is added to your deposit every three months and is paid to you at maturity.
Senior citizens get an extra half percent, so their rate is four point five percent per year.
You can close the deposit early after one year, with a one percent reduction in the interest rate.
You can take a loan against the deposit, up to ninety percent of the deposit amount.
Your deposit is insured by DICGC up to five lakh rupees per depositor per bank.
You can open the deposit at a branch, on the XYZ Bank website, or on the XYZ Bank mobile app.

## SECTION 02: Interest rate

The XYZ Long-Term Fixed Deposit gives an interest rate of four percent per year.
The interest is added to your deposit every three months. This is called quarterly compounding.
Because the interest also earns interest, your money grows faster the longer you stay invested.
The rate is the same for every tenure from twenty years to forty years.
The rate is fixed for the full tenure of the XYZ Long-Term Fixed Deposit once you open it.

## SECTION 03: Tenure (how long the money stays)

The XYZ Long-Term Fixed Deposit has a tenure from twenty years to forty years.
You can choose any whole number of years in this range, for example twenty, twenty-five, thirty, or forty years.
This deposit is made for long-term goals, such as retirement savings or a fund for a child's future.
The interest rate is the same for every tenure in this range.

## SECTION 04: Minimum deposit

The minimum amount to open an XYZ Long-Term Fixed Deposit is ten thousand rupees.
You can deposit more than this amount if you want.
There is no need to deposit in round numbers. Any amount above ten thousand rupees is fine.

## SECTION 05: How interest is paid (payout and compounding)

In the XYZ Long-Term Fixed Deposit, interest is cumulative.
This means the interest is not paid to you every month or every quarter.
Instead, the interest is added to your deposit every three months, and you receive the full amount at maturity.
This is the reason your money grows faster over a long period.
There is no monthly or quarterly payout option in this deposit.

## SECTION 06: Senior citizen benefit

Senior citizens get an extra half percent per year on the XYZ Long-Term Fixed Deposit.
So the rate for senior citizens is four point five percent per year, instead of four percent.
A senior citizen means a person aged sixty years or above.
The customer needs to show an age proof, such as Aadhaar or PAN, to get this benefit.

## SECTION 07: Main benefits and USPs

The XYZ Long-Term Fixed Deposit has these main benefits.
First, steady and safe growth. The rate is fixed at four percent for the full tenure, so it does not change with the market.
Second, long-term compounding. Interest is added every three months, so your money grows faster over twenty to forty years.
Third, safety. Your deposit is insured by DICGC up to five lakh rupees per depositor per bank.
Fourth, emergency support. You can take a loan against the deposit, up to ninety percent of the deposit amount, without breaking it.
Fifth, extra rate for senior citizens. They get an extra half percent per year.
Sixth, easy to open. You can open it at a branch, on the website, or on the mobile app.

## SECTION 08: Who can open it and documents needed

Any adult Indian resident customer of XYZ Bank can open the XYZ Long-Term Fixed Deposit. The person must be eighteen years or older.
New customers can also open it after completing the bank's KYC process.
The documents needed are an identity proof such as Aadhaar or PAN, an address proof, and a passport-size photograph.
The bank executive will tell you the full and final list of documents.

## SECTION 09: How to open the deposit

There are three ways to open the XYZ Long-Term Fixed Deposit.
You can visit any XYZ Bank branch and meet the bank executive.
You can open it on the XYZ Bank website by logging in and choosing the Fixed Deposit option.
You can open it on the XYZ Bank mobile app in the same way.
If you would like help, I can note your name and phone number, and a bank executive will call you back.

## SECTION 10: Closing the deposit early (premature withdrawal)

You can close the XYZ Long-Term Fixed Deposit before maturity, but only after completing one year.
You cannot close the deposit in the first year.
If you close it early, the bank reduces your interest rate by one percent as a penalty.
For example, if you close early, you earn three percent per year instead of four percent for the time your money was deposited.
You get back your deposit plus the reduced interest earned until the closing date.

## SECTION 11: Loan against the deposit

You can take a loan against your XYZ Long-Term Fixed Deposit without closing it.
The loan can be up to ninety percent of your deposit amount.
This is useful in an emergency, because your deposit keeps growing and you do not have to break it.
The interest rate and the other loan terms will be shared by the bank executive.

## SECTION 12: Tax on the interest

The interest you earn on the XYZ Long-Term Fixed Deposit is taxable as per income tax rules.
The bank may deduct TDS, which means tax deducted at source, when the interest crosses the limit set by the tax rules.
The exact limit and the rate can change, so the bot does not quote them.
Please ask the bank executive or a tax advisor for the current limit and what applies to your case.

## SECTION 13: Safety of the deposit

Your money in the XYZ Long-Term Fixed Deposit is insured by DICGC, which is the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation.
The insurance covers up to five lakh rupees per depositor per bank.
This five lakh rupees includes both your deposit and the interest, across all your accounts in the same bank.
The interest rate is fixed, so it does not go up or down with the market.

## SECTION 14: Examples of returns

These examples are for a deposit of one lakh rupees, with interest added every three months. They are approximate.
At four percent for twenty years, one lakh rupees becomes about 2 lakh 21 thousand rupees.
At four percent for twenty-five years, one lakh rupees becomes about 2 lakh 70 thousand rupees.
At four percent for thirty years, one lakh rupees becomes about 3 lakh 30 thousand rupees.
At four percent for forty years, one lakh rupees becomes about 4 lakh 91 thousand rupees.
For senior citizens at four point five percent, one lakh rupees becomes about 2 lakh 45 thousand rupees in twenty years, and about 5 lakh 99 thousand rupees in forty years.
For any other amount or tenure, the bot must use the returns calculator and must never guess.
The formula is final amount equals deposit multiplied by one plus the rate divided by four, raised to the power of four times the number of years.

## SECTION 15: Common objections and how to answer

Question: Is four percent too low?
Answer: Four percent is lower than some short-term deposits. But this deposit is made for long-term, steady growth. The rate is fixed for the full tenure, your money is insured up to five lakh rupees, and you can take a loan against it in an emergency.

Question: Why not just keep money in a savings account?
Answer: A savings account is good for daily use, but the money does not grow in a planned way. In the XYZ Long-Term Fixed Deposit, your interest is added every three months and your money grows steadily over the long term.

Question: Why is the tenure so long, twenty to forty years?
Answer: This deposit is made for long-term goals like retirement or a child's future. A long tenure gives more time for compounding, which means your interest also earns interest.

Question: What if I need the money suddenly?
Answer: After one year you can close the deposit early with a one percent reduction in the interest rate. You can also take a loan against the deposit up to ninety percent without closing it.

Question: Is my money safe?
Answer: Yes. Your deposit is insured by DICGC up to five lakh rupees per depositor per bank.
